What Is FUE Hair Transplant?

Follicular Unit Extraction is a minimally invasive technique that extracts individual hair follicles from your donor area and implants them where you want new growth.

FUE differs from older surgical methods because there's no linear scar. Instead, our clinical team extracts individual follicles one by one using micro-punches under local anesthesia. Each graft is then carefully implanted in the thinning or balding areas.

The result is natural-looking hair that grows permanently — for life. Most patients see initial growth at 3–4 months and full results by 12–18 months.
