What Is DHI Hair Transplant?

DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) is an advanced variation of FUE that uses a specialized tool called the Choi Implanter Pen. Instead of pre-cutting recipient channels and then implanting grafts, DHI extracts and implants each follicle in one continuous motion.

Key Advantages:

  • No pre-made incisions — channels are created during implantation
  • Precise angle and depth control — each follicle positioned for maximum natural appearance
  • Less scalp trauma — only the Choi Pen creates micro-channels
  • Higher density in targeted areas — ideal for hairlines and existing hair zones

DHI or FUE — What's the Difference for You?

FUE is recommended for:

  • Larger restoration areas (temples + crown)
  • First-time procedures covering broad scalp areas
  • Patients on a tighter budget (FUE starts at $2,345 vs DHI from $3,200)

DHI is recommended for:

  • Hairline precision and high-density zones
  • Adding density to areas where existing hair remains
  • Patients who prefer not to shave the recipient area
  • Eyebrow or beard restoration

Frequently Asked Questions — DHI Hair Transplant Tijuana

What is DHI hair transplant?
DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) is an advanced technique that uses the Choi Implanter Pen to extract and implant follicles simultaneously, allowing more precise angle control and higher density than standard FUE. Each follicle is positioned with exact depth and direction in one continuous motion.
How much does DHI cost at IHTC Tijuana?
DHI starts at $3,200 USD — significantly less than the $10,000–$30,000 typical at US clinics for the same procedure. The final cost depends on the number of grafts required, which is determined during your free consultation.
Is DHI better than FUE?
Not universally — they serve different needs. DHI offers more precision for hairlines and dense areas; FUE is often preferred for larger coverage areas. Our clinical team recommends the right technique based on your specific hair loss pattern and goals.
Does DHI require shaving the head?
Not necessarily for the recipient area. DHI can be performed in existing hair without shaving those zones, making it ideal if you want to add density without losing your current hair. The donor area (back of scalp) typically requires trimming for graft extraction.
How long does DHI take?
DHI typically takes 6–10 hours depending on the number of grafts needed. Most patients return home the same day. You'll receive full post-operative instructions to ensure proper healing.
When do DHI results appear?
Initial growth appears at 3–4 months. You'll see continued growth and thickening over 6–12 months. Full, permanent, natural-looking results appear at 12–18 months as the transplanted follicles establish their growth cycle.
Is it safe to have a DHI procedure done in Tijuana?
Yes — when the procedure is performed by a board-certified physician in a properly equipped surgical facility. Dr. Yáñez holds a verified Cédula Profesional and operates under COFEPRIS standards.
