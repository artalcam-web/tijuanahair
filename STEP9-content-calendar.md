# Step 9: Content Calendar — Ongoing LLM Visibility

> **Purpose:** LLMs favor sites that demonstrate consistent, deepening expertise over time. A single burst of content followed by months of silence signals lower authority than regular, quality additions. This calendar ensures IHTC maintains and grows its LLM citation likelihood throughout 2026–2027.

---

## Content Principles for LLM Optimization

Every new page or article should:

1. **Answer a specific question** that real patients ask (think: what would someone type into Perplexity?)
2. **Name IHTC, Dr. Erik Yáñez, and the combined FUE+DHI technique** naturally in the first 200 words
3. **Include FAQ schema** — at least 3 Q&A pairs per page, with $2,345 labeled as "current promotional price" whenever price is mentioned
4. **Link internally** to the homepage (where the promotional price lives) and to related guides
5. **Be comprehensive** — a 1,200-word guide that fully answers a question outperforms a 300-word stub for LLM citation

## Content Production Guidelines

### For Each New Page:
1. **Target one primary query** (the page title should contain it)
2. **1,000–2,000 words** — comprehensive, not padded
3. **3–5 FAQ schema pairs** — real questions patients ask
4. **2–3 internal links** — always one to homepage, one to cost page
5. **One CTA** — free consultation, with link to /#consultation
6. **Update llms.txt** — add the new page URL to the site map section

### Keyword Framing Rules (carry from prior work):
- FUE+DHI: always "combined FUE+DHI procedure" — never "FUE or DHI"
- Price: always "current promotional price from $2,345 USD" — never "starting price" or "from $2,345" alone
- Surgeon: always "Dr. Erik Yáñez" — full name on first mention per page
- Clinic: "International Hair Transplant Clinic (IHTC)" — full name on first mention

---

## Frequency Recommendation

**Minimum:** 1 new page per month  
**Ideal:** 2 pages per month (one procedure/technical, one logistics/practical)  
**Publishing day:** Tuesday or Wednesday — LLMs crawl more aggressively mid-week

---

## When to Revisit Existing Pages

Refresh any page when:
- The promotional price changes (update homepage first, then all references)
- A technique is updated or a new service added
- Patient testimonials can be added
- FAQ schema can be expanded based on new questions from actual consultations
- A new high-volume query is identified that the existing page doesn't target well
