How much does a hair transplant cost in Tijuana at IHTC?

FUE starts at $2,345 USD. Most cases fall between $2,345–$4,500 depending on graft count. Your free consultation includes a precise estimate of your individual needs and total cost.

What's included in the IHTC price?

The procedure, custom hairline design, post-op care kit, and WhatsApp recovery support — all included, no extras. There are no hidden fees or surprise invoices.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Your cost estimate from the free consultation is your total price. No surprise invoices, no upsells, no additional charges. What you're quoted is what you pay.

A small deposit is required to reserve your procedure date. This deposit is fully applied to your total cost, so you're not paying extra—just securing your booking.

Yes — IHTC accepts payment in USD and MXN. We accept cash and credit card. Currency exchange is handled transparently at the time of payment.
