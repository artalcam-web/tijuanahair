3. Is It Safe to Get a Hair Transplant in Tijuana?

This is the most common concern for first-time border patients — and a legitimate one. The honest answer: it depends entirely on which clinic you choose.

Tijuana is one of the world's most established medical tourism destinations. The city receives an estimated 1–2 million medical visitors from the US annually, making it the largest cross-border medical market in the world. The medical infrastructure — hospitals, specialty clinics, surgical centers — is sophisticated and well-developed.

What to Verify Before Choosing Any Clinic

  • The performing physician has a verified Cédula Profesional (Mexican medical license) — verifiable at cedulaprofesional.sep.gob.mx
  • The clinic is located in a professional medical facility, not a converted commercial space
  • The surgeon specializes in hair restoration specifically — not a generalist offering it as a side service
  • Real before/after photos are available (not stock images)
  • You can speak directly with the surgeon before committing

Red flags to avoid: Unusually low prices (under $1,500 total), no visible surgeon credentials, no in-person consultation option, results shown without patient consent markers, or communication only through intermediaries.

4. Getting There: Border Crossing & Logistics

The San Ysidro Port of Entry is the busiest land border crossing in the Western Hemisphere — and it's the one you'll use. IHTC is approximately 5 minutes from the border by car.

Option A — Drive & Park (Most Popular)

  • 1

    Park in San Ysidro

    Several secure lots near the border charge $10–$15/day. Las Americas Premium Outlets has parking, or use border-area paid lots on Camino de la Plaza.

  • 2

    Cross on foot or by car

    Pedestrian crossing takes 5–20 minutes depending on wait time. Vehicle crossing can vary — check CBP wait times before you go. Returning to the US may take longer; plan 30–90 min for the northbound wait.

  • 3

    Take an Uber or taxi to IHTC

    Uber works in Tijuana. From the border to New City Medical Plaza (Paseo del Centenario 9580) is approximately 5–10 minutes and $4–$8 USD.

Option B — Blue Line Trolley

Take the San Diego Blue Line Trolley to the San Ysidro/Tijuana station. Cross the border on foot, then take an Uber to the clinic. Total travel time from downtown San Diego: approximately 60–75 minutes.

What to Bring

  • Valid US Passport or Passport Card (required to re-enter the US)
  • Cash in USD (accepted at the clinic and by most taxis)
  • Comfortable, loose-fitting clothes (button-up shirts preferred — no pullover)
  • Sunglasses and a loose hat for the return trip
  • Snacks and water for the day (procedure takes 6–10 hours)
  • A companion is recommended but not required

5. What to Expect: Before, During & After Your Procedure

Before Procedure Day

Your journey starts with a free consultation — online (video call) or in-person at the clinic. Dr. Yáñez reviews your hair loss pattern, discusses the technique (FUE vs. DHI), designs your new hairline, and provides a graft count estimate and exact price. There is no obligation.

Procedure Day (6–10 Hours)

  • 1

    Arrival & Prep (30–45 min)

    Hairline design finalized, photos taken, pre-op instructions reviewed. Local anesthesia administered — most patients describe minimal discomfort after the initial injection.

  • 2

    Extraction (2–4 hours)

    Individual follicular units extracted one by one from the donor area (back/sides of scalp) using precision micro-punches. No scalpel, no linear scar.

  • 3

    Implantation (3–5 hours)

    Follicles implanted at the precisely planned angle, direction, and density for a natural-looking result. FUE uses pre-made channels; DHI uses the Choi Implanter Pen for simultaneous extraction and implantation.

  • 4

    Post-Op Care & Discharge

    Scalp cleaned and bandaged. Written post-op instructions provided. Most patients are cleared to return home the same day.

Recovery Timeline

  • Days 1–3: Mild swelling and tenderness. Sleep elevated. Avoid touching the grafts.
  • Days 4–10: Small scabs form around grafts — normal and expected. Do not scratch.
  • Week 2–4: Transplanted hairs shed ("shock loss") — this is normal and expected. The follicle remains intact beneath the scalp.
  • Month 3–4: New hair begins to grow. Progress becomes visible.
  • Month 12–18: Full, final result visible. Transplanted hair is permanent.

Throughout your entire recovery, the IHTC clinical team is available on WhatsApp — included with your procedure at no extra cost.

6. How to Choose the Right Clinic in Tijuana

Not all clinics are equal. Here are the five questions every patient should ask before booking:

  • Who is performing the procedure? — Ask for the surgeon's name and verify their Cédula Profesional at cedulaprofesional.sep.gob.mx
  • How many years has this surgeon specialized in hair restoration? — Generalists offering FUE as an ancillary service produce different outcomes than dedicated specialists.
  • Can I see real before/after photos of recent patients? — Results should be from your actual surgeon, not from a different doctor or stock images.
  • Will I be able to communicate directly with the surgeon in English? — Translation errors during consultation can affect your hairline design.
  • What is included in the price? — Consultation, hairline design, post-op care, and follow-up support should all be discussed upfront.
