What to Expect — Your Results Timeline

Month 1: Shedding Phase

The transplanted hair sheds in the first 2–4 weeks. This is completely normal — the follicles remain under the scalp and will regrow.

Month 3: First Growth Signs

Fine, thin hair begins to emerge. Coverage is still minimal but follicle activity confirms success.

Month 6: Noticeable Progress

Significant improvement in coverage. Hair thickens and texture normalizes.

Month 12: Near-Final Results

Most patients see 80–90% of their final result by month 12.

Month 18: Full Results

Complete, permanent results. Hair grows naturally for life and can be cut, styled, and treated like native hair.

Every patient's timeline varies slightly. Your clinical team will guide you through each phase via WhatsApp.

Why IHTC Results Look Natural

Custom Hairline Design

Before any procedure, Dr. Yáñez designs the hairline specifically for your face shape, age, and anatomy. Hairlines that frame the face correctly are the foundation of a natural result.

Angle & Direction Precision

Every follicle is implanted at the correct angle and direction to match your natural hair growth pattern. This is what separates a natural result from an obvious one.

FUE & DHI Techniques

Both techniques avoid linear scarring and preserve the natural look of the donor area. DHI adds extra precision for high-density zones.

Individual Follicular Units

Grafts contain 1–4 hairs each, transplanted as they naturally grow — not in plugs or rows. The result blends seamlessly with existing hair.
