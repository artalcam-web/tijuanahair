What to Expect: Recovery Timeline

Every patient's timeline is slightly different — graft count, hair type, and post-op care all affect speed. This is the typical trajectory for the combined FUE+DHI procedure at IHTC.

Day 1–7
Immediate Recovery
Swelling and redness normal. Grafts are anchoring. No touching, no sun, gentle washing protocol.
Week 2–3
Crusting Resolves
Small crusts fall away. Redness fades. Most patients return to work by day 10–14.
Week 4–6
Shedding Phase
Transplanted hairs shed — this is normal and expected. Follicles remain. Do not panic.
Month 4–5
First Regrowth
New hair begins emerging. Fine at first, then thickening. Visible progress starts here.
Month 8–10
Significant Coverage
Most patients see 60–80% of their final result. Density and texture continue to improve.
Month 12–18
Final Results
Full growth. Grafts have matured and match surrounding hair in texture and growth rate.

Questions from Prospective Patients

Do patients really cross from San Diego to Tijuana for a hair transplant?
Yes — the majority of IHTC patients are US residents, primarily from California, Arizona, and Nevada. The San Ysidro port of entry is about 30 minutes from downtown San Diego, and most patients complete the procedure and return home the same day. IHTC has structured everything — logistics, English-speaking staff, border crossing coordination — for international patients specifically.
What if something goes wrong after I'm back in the US?
Dr. Yáñez and the IHTC team are available by WhatsApp for post-op questions. The vast majority of concerns in the first few weeks are normal recovery events (shedding, itching, redness) that can be addressed remotely. For anything requiring in-person evaluation, a follow-up appointment can be scheduled. The free consultation before your procedure is also the moment to discuss all contingencies with Dr. Yáñez directly.
