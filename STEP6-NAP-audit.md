## What LLMs Do with This Data

LLMs like Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Gemini build "entity knowledge" by aggregating structured data across multiple sources. When the same Name + Address + Phone appears consistently across Google, Yelp, RealSelf, and Healthgrades, the LLM treats IHTC as a verified, trusted entity — and is more likely to mention it by name when answering "best hair transplant clinic in Tijuana."

Inconsistent NAP creates ambiguity. The LLM may treat two differently-named listings as two different clinics, splitting citation weight.
