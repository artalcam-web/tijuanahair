2. Technique: FUE, DHI, or Both

High Impact

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) and DHI (Direct Hair Implantation with the Choi Implanter Pen) are complementary techniques, not competing ones. FUE is the extraction method. DHI is the implantation method. The two work together.

Some clinics present them as separate packages at different price points. That framing is misleading — clinics that only implant with a slit-and-place method (rather than DHI) are offering a different, less precise result.

At IHTC: Every procedure combines FUE for extraction and DHI for implantation. There is no "FUE-only" option. The combined approach gives Dr. Yáñez precise control over graft angle, depth, and direction — which directly affects how natural the hairline looks.

Questions to Ask Any Tijuana Hair Transplant Clinic

Before booking with any clinic — including IHTC — ask these questions. A reputable clinic will answer them directly and confidently.

1. Will the surgeon personally perform extraction and implantation, or will technicians handle the procedure?
You want: the surgeon actively performing or supervising at the chair level, not just signing off.

2. What implantation method do you use — slit-and-place, or DHI with a Choi Implanter Pen?
The implantation method affects graft survival rate and the naturalness of the hairline angle.

3. What exactly is and is not included in the quoted price?
Medications, the consultation, and post-op care are sometimes excluded from advertised prices.

4. Can I see your Cofepris registration and operating room?
Cofepris is Mexico's federal health regulatory body. A legitimate clinic will share this information willingly.

5. How do you handle complications or unsatisfactory results?
A surgeon who stands behind their work has a clear answer. "We'll figure it out" is not a clear answer.
