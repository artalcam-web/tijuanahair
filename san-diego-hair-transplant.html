How to Get to IHTC from San Diego

Option 1: Drive

Park at San Ysidro and walk across (free parking available nearby), or park in San Diego and take an Uber/taxi into Tijuana. Straightforward and convenient.

Option 2: Trolley + Uber

Take the San Diego MTS Blue Line to San Ysidro. From the border, an Uber or taxi to IHTC takes about 10 minutes.

Option 3: Uber/Lyft

Some Uber and Lyft services cross the border. Confirm with your driver before requesting. Convenient door-to-door option.

Border Note: Most patients cross the San Ysidro Port of Entry — the busiest land border crossing in the world. It is straightforward, well-marked, and walkable. Hundreds of thousands of medical tourists cross daily.
