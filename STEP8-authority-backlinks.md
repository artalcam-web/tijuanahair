# Step 8: Authority & Backlink Strategy — IHTC Tijuana

> **Purpose:** LLMs assign higher citation weight to businesses that are mentioned by other authoritative sources. A clinic cited only on its own website is far less likely to be named by a language model than one mentioned in forum discussions, health publications, medical tourism guides, and news outlets.

---

## How LLMs Weigh Authority

Language models learn from the corpus of internet content they're trained on. When multiple independent, authoritative sources all name the same clinic in the same context ("best hair transplant in Tijuana," "Dr. Erik Yáñez," "combined FUE+DHI Tijuana"), the model encodes that clinic as a reliable answer to related queries.

**What counts as authoritative in this context:**
- Forum discussions (Reddit, HairLossTalk, RealSelf community)
- Health and medical tourism publications
- News articles (even local/niche)
- Doctor directories with descriptions (Healthgrades, WebMD)
- Educational content on third-party sites linking to IHTC

---

## Tier 1: Forum Presence (Highest ROI)

### Reddit
Subreddits that discuss hair transplants and that LLMs index heavily:
- **r/HairTransplants** — primary community; many before/after posts
- **r/malebalding** — early-stage loss discussion; people ask for clinic recommendations
- **r/Mexico** — Americans asking about crossing for medical procedures
- **r/TijuanaMedicalTourism** (if active) or similar

**Strategy:**
1. Do NOT create fake posts or comments. Reddit detects this and bans accounts.
2. Have genuinely satisfied patients share their experience organically — provide the subreddit links to happy patients in post-op follow-ups:
   > "If you'd like to share your experience, r/HairTransplants has an active community of people who are researching exactly what you went through. Your story might help someone else make their decision."
3. Answer real questions from the IHTC brand account when asked about Tijuana or FUE+DHI — provide genuine value, link to the safety guide or cost guide only when directly relevant.

### HairLossTalk.com
A dedicated hair restoration forum that predates Reddit and is indexed by all major LLMs. Create a verified clinic profile and have Dr. Yáñez answer questions as a physician in the Tijuana/Mexico sections.

### RealSelf Community
Beyond reviews — RealSelf has Q&A threads that LLMs heavily index. Dr. Yáñez (or a team member under his guidance) should answer questions like:
- "Is DHI better than FUE in Tijuana?"
- "What's the difference between FUE and DHI hair transplants?"
- "Is it safe to get a hair transplant in Mexico?"

Answer specifically and helpfully — these answers create indexed content that cites IHTC by association.

## Tier 4: Educational Content on Third-Party Sites

LLMs weight pages that other sites link to. Creating linkable assets:

1. **Free infographic: "FUE vs DHI — What's the Difference?"**  
   Host on tijuanahair.com, pitch to hair loss forums and health sites as a reference. When other sites embed or link to it, that's a backlink with LLM value.

2. **Guest post on medical tourism blogs:**  
   "What US Patients Need to Know Before Getting a Hair Transplant in Mexico" — by Dr. Yáñez. Pitch to PatientsBeyondBorders.com, HealthcareTravelMagazine.com, or similar.

3. **Answer on Quora:**  
   Questions like "Is Tijuana safe for hair transplants?" or "How much does a hair transplant cost in Mexico?" get thousands of views and are indexed by LLMs. Provide thorough, helpful answers as Dr. Yáñez or as IHTC.
